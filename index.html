Welcome to Astralis

Project Polaro automation engineered for efficiency and precision.

Quick Start

• Ensure Roblox FPS is capped at 60 or lower.
• Adjust your MVP filter if you use one.
• Open Menu → Bag → Pokéballs (REQUIRED).
• Close the in-game chat (upper-left corner).
• Stand in your chosen grass patch.
• Start the macro from Astralis.
• Run the ;hunt command in-game.

How It Works

• Reads the encounter banner text via OCR in your configured Target region.
• Parses “holding a …” to extract the item name and matches it against your Targets list.
• If the matched item is enabled, Astralis opens the bag, selects your chosen ball, then clicks Use.
• If not enabled (or no item is present), it clicks Run.

OCR & Regions

• Roblox must stay visible (not minimized). A portion of the window must be on-screen.
• Use a consistent window size. Windowed or borderless/fullscreen-windowed both work.
• Windows Display Scale of 100% is recommended for crisp OCR.
• Use the Toggle Regions hotkey to place Target/HUD rectangles; verify bag/run thresholds in Settings.

Troubleshooting

• “Roblox not visible”: keep the window not minimized and at least partly on-screen; avoid exclusive fullscreen; bring it to the active desktop/monitor.
• Misreads (OCR): re-place the Target region so the banner text is fully inside (add ~10–20 px height); use windowed/borderless; avoid overlays on the banner.
• Bag/Run not detected: re-capture bag/run images and tune thresholds (±0.05); verify HUD sub-regions (bag_hud_region/run_hud_region) are correctly positioned.
• Clicks wrong place: re-run region setup after any resize; reset use_region/ball_region; ensure the ball label text matches the image you expect.
• High CPU: raise poll_ms; shrink Target/HUD regions; turn off verbose logging; limit Roblox FPS or close heavy apps.

How the Farm Bot Works
• Talks to the NPC automatically.
• Confirms dialogs by pressing Yes / Next when needed.
• Starts the battle by clicking Fight.
• Uses the selected move slot every battle.
• Detects learn-move prompts and always clicks No.
• Wraps up by tapping through Next screens, then repeats.
Best Practices
• Always preview your click points before starting.
• Pick a move slot that deals damage.
• Avoid resizing or moving the Roblox window while farming.
• Stand on the left side of the cloning tube while farming.
• Don't have Pokémon in your party that are lower than level 100.
Troubleshooting
• If clicks look misaligned, adjust the Click Points on the left.
• If the bot clicks the wrong move, double-check the selected slot number.
• If "No" on learn-move is missed, expand the OCR region in settings.
• Make sure Roblox stays visible — minimized windows can't be clicked.
• Press F7 to close Astralis entirely if the bot gets stuck or misbehaves.

Astralis Bot — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2025-09-05

Summary: We collect the minimum data required to operate item-DM notifications and commands.

Data Collected:
• Discord IDs (user/server), usernames at time of interaction.
• One-time link token to associate your PC with your Discord account.
• Notification preferences (e.g., items you opt into) if you choose to use them.
• Basic operational logs (timestamps, minimal HTTP metadata) for reliability and abuse prevention.
We do not collect or store your Discord user token.

Use of Data: Operate the Bot, deliver notifications you requested, prevent abuse, and debug issues.

Legal Basis: Legitimate interests to run the service; consent where required.

Retention: Link tokens and preferences until you run /unlink or after 90 days of inactivity. Logs retained up to 30–90 days, then deleted or aggregated.

Sharing: No sale of data. Infrastructure providers (hosting/logging) may process data under contract.

Your Controls: Use /unlink to break the link and stop DMs. Contact us for data questions or deletion.

Children: Intended for users permitted by Discord’s terms.

Security: We apply reasonable safeguards but no system is perfectly secure.
